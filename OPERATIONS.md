# Operations and safety boundary

The launcher starts only installed services and will not mutate a database or terminate a port owner. Bootstrap, migration, and guarded demo seed are explicit scripts.

`/api/matters` supplies tenant-isolated intake, consent, versioned evidence hashes, cited deadlines, professional decisions, appeals, and append-only history. It does not calculate authoritative legal deadlines or make eligibility decisions. Document/OCR storage, identity, calendar, e-signature, court/government portals, billing, and notification adapters require contracts, privilege/privacy review, authoritative rule sources, credentials, and end-to-end filing acknowledgements before enablement.
